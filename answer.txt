Some pros of agile development are its enviroment that creates immense flexibility in team work as well as its
ability to create a continuous feedback loop due to continuos collaboration. Some cons include a potential lack of documentation
and also potential project oversights with features being added easilty through the project process.

b. The Scrum Master is more in charge of the process of the project, ensuring that Scrum practices are followed and
workflow is as smooth as possible for the team. The Product Owner is responsible for the outline of the actual product and
the vision and success of the project.
