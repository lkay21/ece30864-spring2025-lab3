a. 
Some pros of agile development are its enviroment that creates immense flexibility in team work as well as its
ability to create a continuous feedback loop due to continuos collaboration. Some cons include a potential lack of documentation
and also potential project oversights with features being added easilty through the project process.

b. The Scrum Master is in charge of all scrum related tasks. For example, JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints,
and meetings. The Product Owner on the other hand sets the direction for the team, handes
release management and stakeholder management.

c.  The three meetings are the Sprint Planning Meeting, the Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting and the Sprint Retrospective Meeting.

The Sprint Planning Meeting Includes:
- Determines what to complete in the upcoming sprint
- Move stories in sprint scope from backlog to current sprint

Dail Scrum/Standup Meeting Includes:
- Short meeting that happens every day of the sprint
- Team members discuss work completed and wkr plan for day

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
- Occurs at the end of the sprint
- Discuss work complete and move incomplete work to backlog

d. Task: A unit of work that can be tracked through a workflow (bug to an epic)
   Story: Software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences
   Epic: Captures a large body of work that can be broked down into several smaller stories
   Bug: Smallest unit of work, impedes progress in a sprint
